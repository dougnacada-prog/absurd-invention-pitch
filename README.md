A program that writes one sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance — a phone charger that unplugs itself and crawls under the bed whenever you reach for your phone after midnight, so your doomscrolling dies of battery starvation — to /output/entry.txt, the whole entry for make.fun's One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch bounty. One sentence, 160 characters: the laugh comes from the invention itself, and the fix is silly yet weirdly plausible.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
